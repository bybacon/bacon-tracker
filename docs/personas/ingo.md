# Ingo

## Demographics
- 44 years old
- works as a software developer who runs three small products at once, and is their product owner too

## Behaviors
- keeps the board open in a browser tab next to the editor, and the menu bar app running all day
- commits to a short backlog on Monday morning and pulls from the top of it for the rest of the week
- writes stories and decision records in the editor, moves them on the board
- reviews every change as a pull request, including the ones that only touch stories
- hands well-described stories to Jean and reviews what comes back

## Needs & Goals
- see at a glance what is next in every project, without logging in to anything
- keep the project's history in git beside the code - no separate service, no database
- never lose a story or a decision to a merge, a typo or a bad drag
- remember months later why something was decided
- trust that whatever Jean changed shows up in the diff like any other change
